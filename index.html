Same sentence, 2.3× fewer forward passes

Every LLM pays a full forward pass for every token, even the obvious ones. My entropy-adaptive decoding reads the model's own uncertainty and, when it's confident, commits up to four tokens in a single pass. Fewer passes means less GPU compute per response, 2.1× faster generation, and cheaper serving at scale. Below is a simplified illustration.
